Categories and sources

Consumer health data includes the medication names, instructions, schedules, reviewed prescription-label facts, inventory and refill details, appointments, recorded Taken or Skipped actions, check-ins and correction notes you choose to provide. Associated account identifiers, health-consent history, export-revision information and family-sharing permissions link this information to your account and choices.

Sources are information and actions you provide in the app, label details you review and choose to save, your and your family members’ connection and permission actions, and health information you choose to include in a support message. Sign-in and subscription providers supply associated identity and access information. Prescription image recognition is temporary and on-device; the scanner does not upload the image. Extracted details you save become cloud health records.

Purposes

We process these records to save and synchronize your routine, show your recorded history, support corrections and account exports, enforce your consent, and show only permitted family activity. Health processing and family sharing are separate choices. SilverParent does not use health information for advertising.

SilverParent is an organizational tool, not an emergency service, medical advice or proof that a person is safe or has actually taken a medication. Live family alerts are not available in this release.

Sharing and recipients

Supabase hosts account-linked health information and related functions. Family members with an accepted, authorized connection can see permitted activity categories, statuses and times, not your full medication record. Buying a family subscription does not override your permissions. If you include health information in a support message, Gmail processes that message. We do not provide health information to advertising networks or data brokers. Any legally required disclosure is limited to its applicable purpose.

The app’s primary database is hosted in Ohio, United States. Using SilverParent from Canada involves processing outside Canada. Service providers may process operational or support information in other locations under their applicable arrangements. Foreign laws may permit access by courts, law-enforcement agencies or other authorities. A US database location does not mean that every provider operation or copy remains in the United States.

Your choices and privacy rights

Use Settings > Health data privacy to manage health-processing consent, add correction notes or export your account’s eight available sections. Withdrawal stops new cloud routine saves and family sharing, but does not automatically erase earlier records or disable existing on-device reminders. Reacceptance does not revive revoked family connections. Revoke family connections separately as needed. Use Settings > Delete account for verified account deletion. Copies you or a recipient save outside the app remain outside our control.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of personal information, correct it, withdraw consent, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, or appeal a decision. Not every statutory right applies to every person. This notice does not reduce rights you have under applicable law.

Use the in-app controls above or email euainteractive.support@gmail.com. State the type of request and include only enough information to explain it. We verify account ownership through an authenticated in-app or other appropriate secure process before disclosing or changing account information. Never send a password, login code or authentication token by email.

We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law and explain any permitted extension or lawful limitation. To request review of a refusal, reply to the decision or email the privacy contact with your request reference and why you disagree. You may also contact the privacy regulator or other competent authority available under the law that applies to you.

Retention and deletion

Account-linked health records, consent history, correction notes and export revisions remain until removal through available controls or account deletion. Removing a schedule does not erase old dose history. Necessary status/recovery records have a maximum scheduled retention of seven days from primary deletion completion or cancellation of an unaccepted request, with earlier removal when no longer needed. Unfinished primary requests remain protected until resolved. Encrypted Apple-token retention and revocation retry are limited to seven days from acceptance, with earlier removal when no longer needed and no further use after expiry. Pauses or outages can delay physical cleanup; affected records remain restricted and cleanup resumes with service availability. Provider logs and copies and support correspondence are separate; support is retained only as needed for the request and legal obligations, with operator-reviewed minimization.
