Who we are and what the app does

SilverParent is operated by Edgar Acevedo. Privacy and support contact: euainteractive.support@gmail.com.

SilverParent helps adults organize medication routines, record their own medication actions and check-ins, and share selected activity with family members they authorize.

SilverParent is an organizational tool, not an emergency service, medical advice or proof that a person is safe or has actually taken a medication. Live family alerts are not available in this release.

Information we collect and its sources

We store the medication details you save, including names, instructions, selected times and days, pause settings, reviewed prescription-label information, and optional pill inventory and refill details. We also store appointment names, dates and reminder settings, the Taken or Skipped actions you record, and your check-ins. These records are associated with your account.

Prescription-label recognition runs on your device using Apple Vision. The scanner processes the image and recognized text temporarily; it does not upload, save or log the prescription image. You must review the recognized details before saving them. Details you choose to save become part of your account-linked medication record. Selecting an existing photo does not remove the original from your photo library.

Sign in with Apple provides an identity token to Supabase authentication. The app does not request your name or email address from Apple. Account and session identifiers associate your records with the correct account. Session material and account-specific offline copies are stored in the device Keychain.

Supabase authentication records can retain an email address supplied with sign-in information, including an Apple private relay address when provided, as part of your account. Supabase authentication and security logs can include account identifiers, sign-in and session events, IP addresses and technical request information such as user-agent strings. These records support authentication, service operation and security.

The app does not access your address book or GPS location. Camera, photo and notification permissions are optional. Where remote notifications are used, delivery can involve an app-specific device token and related delivery records. This does not mean that live family alerts are available.

Apple handles subscription checkout. We receive and verify product and transaction identifiers, subscription status and expiry, and an account-association identifier to manage subscription access. SilverParent does not ask you to enter payment-card details. Deleting an account removes its app-side purchase associations; it does not guarantee that an old subscription can be restored to a newly created account.

Account-linked health-privacy records include consent choices, notice and purpose versions, the notice digest, language, consent generation, acceptance or withdrawal times, correction notes and export-revision information. We use these records to apply and document your choices, preserve the context of corrections and produce an account-scoped export. They are not an anonymous consent ledger.

If you contact euainteractive.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Google’s Gmail service handles these messages. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens or a complete medical history. Support messages are separate from your in-app account records.

For support, send only what is needed to explain your request. We do not ask for unnecessary sensitive information. Do not send passwords, login codes or authentication tokens, and remove unnecessary health information from screenshots.

How we use information

We use information to provide the functions you choose: sign-in, cloud saving and synchronization of your routine, reminders and recorded history, permitted family activity, subscription access, privacy choices and requests, and support. Technical operational records help providers operate and secure their services. We do not use health information for advertising or provide it to advertising networks or data brokers.

Who receives information

Supabase hosts authentication, the database and server functions for account-linked app information. Apple provides sign-in and App Store purchasing and keeps its own purchase records. Google’s Gmail service handles support correspondence. GitHub Pages hosts this privacy and support website. These services process the information needed for their roles; family members receive only the activity you authorize. Buying a family subscription does not override the parent’s sharing permissions.

Family sharing requires an accepted connection and your permission for the selected categories. We store connection identifiers, invitation information, permissions and consent history to enforce those choices. Family access is limited to permitted activity categories, statuses and times; it does not provide your full medication records. You can revoke a connection. Revocation stops future authorized access through that connection but cannot erase information a recipient has already seen or copied.

The app’s primary database is hosted in Ohio, United States. Using SilverParent from Canada involves processing outside Canada. Service providers may process operational or support information in other locations under their applicable arrangements. Foreign laws may permit access by courts, law-enforcement agencies or other authorities. A US database location does not mean that every provider operation or copy remains in the United States.

This privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices. Do not post health information, account credentials or support requests in a public GitHub issue.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. We limit legally required or active-dispute records to the information needed for that purpose.

Your choices, consent and copies

Open Settings > Health data privacy in the app to review health-processing consent, withdraw consent, add correction notes and export your account data. Open Settings > Delete account to start account deletion. These are the navigation labels used here; labels elsewhere in the app may be in English.

Declining health-processing consent prevents the pending health information from being uploaded. Declining does not erase records already stored. Family sharing is a separate choice and is not authorized by signing in, purchasing a subscription or accepting cloud-health processing.

Withdrawing cloud-health processing consent stops new cloud routine saves and family sharing. Existing stored records are not automatically deleted, and previously configured on-device reminders continue until you turn them off. Reaccepting health processing does not reactivate revoked family connections: new family permission is required. Export, correction and account-deletion choices remain separate.

You can edit or remove medication schedules and appointments, revoke family connections, and manage camera, photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Removing a medication schedule does not erase its previously recorded dose history. Canceling an Apple subscription and deleting a SilverParent account are separate actions.

The account-only export covers eight sections: medications, appointments, medication responses, check-ins, health-consent history, correction notes, app-side purchase records and parent-sharing permissions. It does not include another family member’s account, support messages, provider logs or every operational record. For a broader access request, use the privacy contact below. A correction note supplements the original medication response; it does not change that response or the pill count. Export-revision information supports a consistent export.

You control where you save or share an export. Copies outside the app, including copies retained by another person, are outside SilverParent’s control.

Retention

Account-linked health information, health-consent history, correction notes and export-revision records are retained to provide the service until removed through an available control or account deletion. Removing a medication schedule does not remove its past dose history. Family connections, permissions and app-side purchase associations are retained as needed for their functions and are included in account deletion.

When primary deletion is completed, or an unaccepted deletion request is cancelled, direct account and session links are removed from the limited status and recovery record. Necessary recovery records have a maximum scheduled retention of seven days from completion or cancellation and are removed earlier when no longer needed. The recovery window is not extended for other uses. These records may remain linkable and are not treated as anonymous. An unfinished primary deletion request remains protected until it is resolved.

If an encrypted Apple authorization token is available for revocation, its maximum retention and retry window is seven days from acceptance of the deletion request. The token is removed earlier when it is no longer needed; any remaining token is cleared through cleanup when that window expires. Expiry stops further use of that token, even if Apple authorization cleanup still needs attention. This window is not a promise that primary account deletion or Apple revocation will finish within seven days.

Scheduled cleanup depends on the service being available. A project pause or outage can delay physical cleanup; affected records remain restricted and are cleared when processing resumes. Unfinished deletion requests are not discarded because time has passed. We review stalled requests, resolve the cause, and continue the deletion process. You can check protected status in the app or contact privacy support. This is not an automatic external-alert or emergency service.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. The operator reviews it to minimize unnecessary information. Support messages are separate from in-app account records; account deletion does not automatically erase the email conversation.

Minimized records needed for a legal obligation or an active dispute are held only for the applicable purpose and period. This is not a reason to keep an entire health history or a permanent purchase-ownership record after account deletion.

Provider technical and security logs, backups and operational copies follow separate provider processes and retention arrangements. The account-deletion and recovery periods above do not promise removal of every provider copy at the same time. We do not guarantee an exact deletion instant or a single retention duration for all such copies.

Account deletion

Start at Settings > Delete account. You must confirm your identity and deletion choice in the app. Sending an email alone is not an immediately verified deletion request. Accepted deletion cannot be undone. Use the protected status receipt to check progress; do not email or publicly share its access details. Once accepted, the app revokes sessions and family connections and blocks new account activity while processing deletion.

Deletion removes the account’s stored medication and appointment information, activity, health-consent records, correction notes, export revisions, family links and app-side purchase associations, then the authentication account. It does not preserve an anonymized consent ledger or a permanent purchase-ownership record. A request is not reported complete until primary deletion is confirmed. If your request remains pending, check its protected status in the app or contact euainteractive.support@gmail.com for help. Do not send the status receipt’s access details by email.

Apple authorization revocation is handled separately and may require action in your Apple account. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription, delete Apple’s own purchase records, or erase copies already saved or seen by another person. You can manage or cancel your subscription with Apple before deletion, but subscription cancellation is not a condition for deleting your SilverParent account.

Privacy requests and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of personal information, correct it, withdraw consent, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, or appeal a decision. Not every statutory right applies to every person. This notice does not reduce rights you have under applicable law.

Use the in-app controls above or email euainteractive.support@gmail.com. State the type of request and include only enough information to explain it. We verify account ownership through an authenticated in-app or other appropriate secure process before disclosing or changing account information. Never send a password, login code or authentication token by email.

We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law and explain any permitted extension or lawful limitation. To request review of a refusal, reply to the decision or email the privacy contact with your request reference and why you disagree. You may also contact the privacy regulator or other competent authority available under the law that applies to you.

Security

We use account authentication and permissions to limit access to account information and family activity. Session material and account-specific offline copies use the device Keychain. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee that any device, transmission or storage system is completely secure. Protect your device and account, and contact us if you believe your information has been accessed improperly.

Adults and children

SilverParent is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Do not create an account for a child or submit a child’s health information. If you believe a child’s personal information has been provided, contact the privacy email so it can be reviewed and handled appropriately.

Changes to this notice

We identify this notice by its effective date and policy version. If the handling of personal information changes, we will update the notice and provide any notice or renewed consent required by applicable law before the relevant change takes effect. A notice update alone does not authorize a new incompatible health-data purpose or expanded family sharing.
